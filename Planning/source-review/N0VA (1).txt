How brand variants work

" Each Store Manager account is registered to one outlet; the outletˇs brand decides the order
form and receiving options. Managers never choose a brand.

" Fresh (OUT010) is the full walkthrough. Style and Tech show only the screens that differ: Place
order and Receive.

" Style: weekly order in cartons, ambient only, mall delivery slot.

" Tech: pallets/crates with declared value and fragile flags; receiving adds ˝Accept with
reservation˛ with a note and/or photo.

" Dashboard, Order status, Deferred, History & issues, Notifications and Settings are shared
across all brands.

" Products and prices are sample data (see Components ™ Sample product catalogue). Basket
totals match real orders in the dataset.
